Purpose of our content

EasyTools explanations and examples are intended to help people understand the available tools, required inputs and typical uses. Examples are illustrative and are not promises of a particular result.

Our aim is to describe the tools as implemented and make important limitations clear. When guidance depends on an external service, its official documentation is the primary reference. Service behavior and applicable requirements can change over time.

Users should verify consequential calculations and document outputs against their own requirements. See the Disclaimer for the limits of these utilities.

Automated and AI-generated results

Tool results are generated automatically and are not individually reviewed by an editor or professional. The AI Text Rewriter uses an external AI service; its suggestions may contain mistakes and should be reviewed before use.

Information about that processing is provided in the Privacy Policy.

Advertising spaces are labeled separately from tool controls and explanatory content. An advertisement or external link does not imply that EasyTools has independently verified the advertiser's claims.

Advertising scripts remain blocked until a separate consent integration is configured. Tools do not require interaction with an advertisement.

Explanations and policies may be revised as tools change or errors are identified. The update date on a policy page indicates when that page was revised; it is not a guarantee that every external fact was independently verified on that date.
