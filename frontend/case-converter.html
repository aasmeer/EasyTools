What is this tool?

The EasyTools Case Converter changes the capitalisation of text without requiring a word processor. Paste a sentence, heading or longer passage and select the transformation you need. It is useful when copied text arrives in capitals, when a label needs consistent casing, or when you want to tidy a draft before editing it further. The conversion takes place in the text box, so keep a copy of your original if you want to compare versions. This utility changes letter case; it does not rewrite the meaning or check grammar.

Features

  • Switch the entire passage to uppercase or lowercase with a single action.
  • Try Title Case, Sentence case or Capitalize Words for different presentation needs.
  • Use toggle case to reverse the case of letters in the current text.
  • Check the displayed word and character counts, then copy or clear the text.

Benefits

Consistent capitalisation makes headings, lists and short labels easier to scan. For instance, changing 'MEETING NOTES' to lowercase can prepare it for reuse within a sentence, while uppercase can make a short identifier consistent with neighbouring labels. Working directly in the browser avoids repeatedly moving text through a desktop editor. The most useful workflow is to apply the broad transformation first, then correct exceptions such as product names, initials, abbreviations and words whose capitalisation carries meaning.

How to use

  1. Paste or type the passage into the labelled text area.
  2. Choose the case button that matches the desired transformation.
  3. Read the converted text and correct names, acronyms or other exceptions manually.
  4. Select Copy Text to reuse the result, or Clear Text to begin a new passage.

Examples and limitations

Different publishers use different title styles, especially for short words and words after punctuation. An automatic case rule cannot infer every editorial preference. A phrase such as 'an API guide for iPhone users' contains an acronym and a product name that deserve review after conversion. Keep such examples in mind when applying a rule to an entire document rather than a single heading.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between uppercase and lowercase?

Uppercase turns letters with case into capitals; lowercase uses their small-letter forms. Numbers and many punctuation marks do not have case and are not transformed in the same way.

Are Title Case and Capitalize Words interchangeable?

They are separate options with potentially different handling of words. Review the actual output against the style you need instead of assuming either option implements a particular publisher's complete style guide.

Will the tool fix spelling or grammar?

No. A capitalisation change does not establish that a sentence is grammatical or correctly spelled. Proofread the result separately, particularly when copied text already contains mistakes.

Can I undo a conversion?

There is no dedicated conversion-history control. Copy your original somewhere safe before a large change, especially when its mixed capitalisation contains names or abbreviations that cannot be reconstructed automatically.

Is the text sent to an AI service?

No. Case conversion happens in your browser and does not require an AI or server-processing request. If you instead choose the related AI Rewriter, that tool has a separate processing disclosure.
