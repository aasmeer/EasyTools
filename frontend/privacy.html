Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how EasyTools may collect, use and handle information when you use our website and online tools.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information when using features such as a contact form. This may include your name, email address, message or other information you choose to submit.

2. Files and Tool Processing

The PDF, image and other browser-based tools process their inputs on your device. The AI Text Rewriter is different: selecting Rewrite Text sends your text and chosen tone and length to the EasyTools server, which sends the text and rewrite instructions to OpenAI. The generated response is returned to your browser.

OpenAI may retain submitted content and responses under its API data policies. Its Responses API has a default application-state retention period of at least 30 days, and separate abuse-monitoring retention can apply. Provider settings and legal requirements can affect retention. EasyTools does not promise zero retention. See OpenAI's API data controls for the provider's current training, retention and data-control policies.

Submit only text you are authorized to share with an external AI service. Avoid passwords, confidential documents and sensitive personal information. Review AI output before relying on it. Analytics preferences do not control this requested AI processing.

3. Automatically Collected Information

When EasyTools is publicly available, technical information may be collected automatically by hosting, analytics or security services.

  • Browser and device type
  • Approximate location based on IP address
  • Pages visited
  • Referral source
  • Website performance information
  • Basic usage and error data

4. Cookies

We store your analytics preference in your browser's local storage for up to 180 days. This preference lets us remember your choice; it is not sent to an analytics provider. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice applies only to the current page. Tools remain available when you decline.

Use the Privacy settings button below the navigation on any page to change your analytics choice. Declining disables further collection by our Google Analytics tag and clears its accessible first-party analytics cookies. Advertising consent is separate and is not granted by this control.

5. Advertising

EasyTools uses Google AdSense to support the website when advertising is enabled. Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this website and other websites. Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to personalize those ads.

Advertising scripts remain blocked until an appropriate consent-provider integration is configured. Where consent is required, ads must wait for the choices recorded by that provider. Analytics permission does not give permission for advertising.

You can manage Google's personalized advertising at Google My Ad Center, learn how Google uses information from partner sites at Google's partner-site privacy information, or opt out of participating vendors' personalized ads through YourAdChoices.

6. Analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you allow analytics, including when a valid saved choice exists. It helps us understand page usage and improve the website. Analytics cookies may last up to 180 days. Google advertising signals and ad-personalization signals are disabled for this tag. You can decline or withdraw permission in Privacy settings.

7. Third-Party Services

EasyTools may rely on third-party services such as hosting providers, analytics services, advertising networks, content delivery networks or external libraries.

8. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect EasyTools and information processed through the website. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

9. Children's Privacy

EasyTools is intended as a general-purpose online utility website. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as EasyTools evolves. Updated versions will be posted on this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us through the EasyTools contact page.
