Cookie Policy

How EasyTools uses browser storage, analytics cookies and privacy choices.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Browser storage and your choice

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by your browser. Local storage is a separate browser feature. EasyTools uses local storage to remember whether you allow or decline analytics for up to 180 days. This preference does not itself send information to Google Analytics.

If browser storage is unavailable, the choice applies only to the current page. Clearing site data removes the saved preference. You can use the tools when you decline analytics.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you allow it, including when you have a valid saved choice. It can collect page-use and device information and set first-party analytics cookies, including _ga and a property-specific _ga_ cookie.

EasyTools configures these cookies with a 180-day lifetime. Continued use may refresh their expiry. Google advertising signals and advertising personalization signals are disabled for this analytics tag.

Changing your choice

Use the Privacy settings button below the navigation to allow or decline analytics. Declining stops further collection by the EasyTools Google Analytics tag and clears the accessible first-party analytics cookies for this property. Withdrawing permission does not recall information already sent.

Your browser settings also let you block or clear cookies and site storage. These settings can affect other websites and their saved preferences.

Advertising is separate

Advertising scripts are currently blocked. Allowing analytics does not authorize advertising cookies. Advertising will require a separately configured consent process before it is enabled.

See the Privacy Policy for advertising information and links to Google's privacy and advertising controls.

Other processing

Browser storage choices do not control information needed to deliver a page or process a request you make. In particular, the AI Text Rewriter sends submitted text to an external AI provider. See AI processing and privacy.

This policy should be read with the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
